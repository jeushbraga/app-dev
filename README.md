## About the Series

**Dragon Ball** is a Japanese *anime and manga* franchise created by **Akira Toriyama**. It follows **Son Goku**, a Saiyan raised on Earth, as he searches for the Dragon Balls and trains to become the strongest fighter in the universe.

## Why It's My Favorite

- Exciting fights and powerful transformations
- Strong themes of **friendship**, *perseverance*, and self-improvement
- Memorable characters and humor
- Goku never stops training and never gives up
